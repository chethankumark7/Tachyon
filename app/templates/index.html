Competition Architecture & Governance Rules

  • Authoritative Backend Truth: Point calculations and rankings are derived exclusively by the Flask backend domain engine. Ranks follow standard competition ranking (1224 schema with tied ranks skipping subsequent values).
  • Audit Ledger Integrity: Every point event recorded is immutable with positive integers and verified achievement reasons.
  • Co-Winners Resolution: In the event of a tie at the highest accumulated score, all tied leaders are recognized as co-leaders.
